01. What is Neo-Brutalism? +
It's a design trend characterized by stark contrasts, sharp geometric shapes, bold typography, and a deliberate avoidance of soft, modern UI conventions like gradients or large border radii.
02. Why use a white theme? +
While dark themes are striking, a white theme significantly improves readability for long-form content. The brutalist elements (thick borders, hard shadows) stand out even more against a stark white canvas.
