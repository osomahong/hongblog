Design Rules Summary

  • Border-radius: 0px — everywhere, no exceptions
  • Borders: black only — 4px (cards) / 2px (buttons, badges)
  • Shadows: hard offset — no blur, no spread beyond offset
  • Colors: 3 primaries — Red #F00, Gold #FFD700, Black #000
  • Hover: translate + shadow collapse — physical press feel
  • Mobile: cards become borderless — transparent bg, no shadow
